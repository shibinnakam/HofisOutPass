📋 School Outpass Guidelines

1. Advance Application: Outpasses can be requested for today or up to 7 calendar days in advance.
2. Timing Rule: If applying for today, the departure time must be strictly in the future (past dates and past times are blocked).
3. Email Notification: Parents receive an automated digital outpass via email once approved by school administration.
4. School Exit Verification: Before leaving school, tell the student's admission number to the class teacher. They will verify the approved outpass, after which you may leave.
