Outpass Guidelines

Please follow these school outpass rules before submitting a request

1

Same-Day Submission

Parents must submit the student outpass request on the same day of departure.

2

Departure Time Window (9:30 AM – 3:30 PM)

Allowed departure time is strictly between 9:30 AM and 3:30 PM during school hours.

3

No Outpass on Sundays

Outpass requests cannot be submitted on Sundays as the school is closed.

4

No Past Date or Time

Do not select a departure date or time that has already passed.
